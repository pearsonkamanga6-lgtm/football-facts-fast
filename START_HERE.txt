WHAT v6.1 FIXES
- Rejects irrelevant search results such as animal pages for “Lion” and unrelated “BG” pages.
- Checks relevance again after opening a page, so a misleading search snippet cannot enter final evidence.
- Keeps rejected URLs visible in the source audit but excludes them from analysis.
- Decodes Bing redirect links before judging the real website/domain.
- Balances the source budget across research questions instead of allowing noisy early queries to consume the whole source limit.
- Uses independent web fixture verification to strengthen team-identity confidence when API-Football is unavailable.
- Does not call a one-domain market phrase “multi-source”.
- Normalizes equivalent market wording before AI-council convergence is calculated.
- Counts repeated Gemini specialist prompts as one underlying Gemini model for unique-model consensus.
- Repairs shortlist rendering when an AI returns market scores but an incomplete shortlist.
- Searches external benchmark sites through the whole search fleet and reads the actual page.
- Adds a local explicit-prediction parser so a clearly labelled website prediction can still be extracted even when Gemini text quota is unavailable.
