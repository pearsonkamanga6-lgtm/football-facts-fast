How should the session start?

Discovery ranking uses structured coverage and public-source availability. Bookmaker odds are deliberately ignored until after the sporting analysis.

The ceiling is 100 agent seats. Research expands in waves and can stop early when independent underlying models converge. The app keeps agent consensus separate from unique-model consensus so repeated specialist prompts cannot fake agreement.
Search engines discover URLs; the engine then opens readable pages, builds an evidence warehouse, removes duplicate content families and keeps researching until the selected depth or saturation threshold is reached.
Facts first, odds last: current squads, fixture identity, statistics, web sources and video evidence are researched before bookmaker prices are fetched. Every realistic market family is screened. Only after the sporting shortlist exists does the app compare available prices and flag any potential value where its estimated fair probability is meaningfully above the market break-even probability.

Research engine setup v6.1

v6.1 keeps the multi-search fleet but adds a strict relevance firewall, cleaner source scoring, rejected-source auditing, semantic council convergence and stronger external-prediction extraction. If you still see an older version after deploy, close the tab and reopen the Render URL.

Add these Environment Variables in Render. Keep all keys on the server, never in the browser:

  • API_FOOTBALL_KEY — structured football facts, predictions and bookmaker odds.
  • TAVILY_API_KEY — fresh web research and source discovery.
  • GEMINI_API_KEY — primary analyst, video reviewer and first council member.
  • GROQ_API_KEY — adds OpenAI GPT-OSS 120B and Qwen 3.8 27B.
  • CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID + CLOUDFLARE_AUTH_TOKEN — optionally adds Meta Llama.
  • OPENROUTER_API_KEY — optional free-router council fallback.
  • APP_PUBLIC_URL — your Render URL for OpenRouter app identification.
  • FOOTBALL_DATA_ORG_KEY — optional free fixture/schedule fallback.
  • SCOREBAT_TOKEN — optional limited free highlight-feed fallback.
  • THESPORTSDB_API_KEY — optional; free V1 key defaults to 123.

Authenticity workflow

Two start modes remain: (1) you give the games, or (2) the app finds data-rich games. Every fixture then follows: DATA COLLECTION → DATA ANALYSIS → INDEPENDENT AI COUNCIL → DATA PRESENTATION → external prediction benchmarks → ODDS/VALUE LAST. Council models receive the same locked evidence pack with prices hidden. Disagreement is preserved instead of forced into fake consensus.
